Good to know

The niacin flush — no need to panic

When you take the B-Vitamin Complex with Niacin, the so-called niacin flush can set in after about 10–15 minutes: a warmth, often a slight tingling or reddening of the skin. This is a known, well-documented effect of niacin — it briefly widens the blood vessels and boosts circulation. Don't be alarmed — the effect is normal, harmless, and fades on its own after a short while.

For me, that exact sensation was an important signal: I read it as a sign that my body — and, the way I felt it, my ear too — was being especially well supplied with blood and nutrients in that moment.

Easing in

If you'd like to ease into the concept carefully, you can start with the B-complex alone — to experience the flush once for yourself while supporting your metabolism with the B-vitamins. And maybe, like me, you'll notice a tangible rise in energy over time.
