What people around the world report

While researching, I kept coming across accounts from people I don’t know—in forums, on Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube, in German, Korean, Russian, and French. I’m collecting them here. They show that people around the world—independently of one another and without knowing each other—report similar experiences with tinnitus and a nutrient-based approach. I can’t judge from the outside exactly what happened in each person’s case—you have to decide for yourself what you make of it.
