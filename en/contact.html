Feel Free to Write to Me

Do you have questions about my story, about the content of this site, or about my path out of tinnitus? Then I’d be glad to hear from you. I read every email myself.

Email Dustin5.mueller87@gmail.com

I reply as quickly as I can—but sometimes it takes a few days because I answer every message myself. Please be a little patient.

A brief, honest note: I share my personal experiences here and am glad to answer questions about what’s written on this site—but I can’t give individual medical advice, and I’m not allowed to. If your tinnitus or hearing loss has just started or come on suddenly, please see an ENT doctor first rather than waiting for my reply. In the acute phase, that’s the most important thing.
