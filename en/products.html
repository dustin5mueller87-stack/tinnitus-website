Good to know

The niacin flush—no need to panic

When you take the B-Vitamin Complex with Niacin, the so-called niacin flush can set in after about 10–15 minutes: a feeling of warmth, often accompanied by slight tingling or reddening of the skin. This is a known, well-documented effect of niacin—niacin briefly widens the blood vessels and boosts circulation. Don’t be alarmed—the effect is normal, harmless, and fades on its own after a short while.

For me, that exact sensation was an important signal: I interpreted it to mean that my body—and therefore, subjectively, my ear as well—had especially good circulation and was well supplied at that moment.

Easing in

If you’d like to start cautiously, you can begin with the B-complex alone—to experience the flush for yourself while also supporting your metabolism with B vitamins. Maybe, like me, you’ll also notice a tangible increase in energy over time.
