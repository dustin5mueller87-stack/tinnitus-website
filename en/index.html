In Short: What Is ATP?

ATP is—put simply—the energy in your cells: the fuel your body uses for strength, stamina, and physical capacity. The lower the level, the closer your cells are to running on empty. My lab value was 0.37—way down in the basement. My physical energy back then was at rock bottom right along with it.

Section 01

Why This Site Exists

I know exactly what it feels like to lie awake at night, unable to switch that sound off. I know what it’s like to hear a doctor tell you, “Learn to live with it.” And I know what it’s like to feel completely alone with a problem no one else can see.

When I first developed tinnitus after a night at a club in 2011, it sent me on an odyssey through ENT practices, forums, and sleepless nights. Four different ENT doctors. None of them could help me. So I started doing my own research—thousands of hours over the course of years. I dove deep into cell biology, cochlear physiology, neurology, and nutritional biochemistry.

In the end, I found my own way. My tinnitus disappeared completely. Audiometry documented clear improvements during my recovery, by which point my tinnitus was already about 80% gone. Years later, I deliberately brought on a second, separate episode of tinnitus to test my model. It, too, disappeared—noticeably faster this time.

I created this site because I can’t keep this knowledge to myself. Not every path works for every person. But if my experiences and research help even one person with tinnitus find hope again, every hour I’ve put into this site was worth it.

Understanding Tinnitus: The Three Main Causes

Not all tinnitus is the same. Based on my experience and research, there are three fundamentally different triggers—and each one calls for a different approach.

Noise-Induced Tinnitus

Clubs, concerts, acoustic trauma, chronic noise exposure—when the ear has been mechanically overloaded, its hair cells get stuck in an energy-starved emergency mode. Here I explain the entire cellular mechanism: what happens at the molecular level, why the tone persists, and why conventional masking therapy can provide short-term relief but, as I understand it, works against the ear’s actual recovery.

→ Go to the Explanation Page: Noise-Induced Tinnitus →

Stress-Induced / Psychosomatic Tinnitus

Not all tinnitus comes from the ear. Long-standing unresolved inner conflicts can generate persistent electrical potentials in the nervous system that stimulate the auditory system from within. Here I describe how that model works. I also explain how working through those conflicts helped me during a separate period of CFS and psychosomatic symptoms. I never had stress-related tinnitus myself.

Medication- and Toxin-Induced Tinnitus (Ototoxicity)

Certain medications, heavy metals, and environmental toxins can chemically attack the hair cells and the auditory nerve from within. Here I explain the mechanisms behind the major ototoxic substances and why this kind of tinnitus is often especially stubborn.
