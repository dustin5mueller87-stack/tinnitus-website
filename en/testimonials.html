What people around the world report

Here I collect publicly accessible reports and personal accounts that I found while researching across various languages, forums, and platforms. They show that people around the world—independently of one another and without knowing each other—report similar experiences with tinnitus and a nutrient-based approach. What effect these posts have in any individual case can’t be judged from a distance—you have to decide for yourself what you make of them.
