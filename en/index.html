A Quick Explanation: What Is ATP?

ATP is—put simply—the energy in your cells: the fuel your body draws on for strength, endurance, and fitness. The lower the value, the closer the cells are to running on empty. My lab value of 0.37 was rock bottom—and my physical energy back then was right down there with it.

Section 01

Why This Page Exists

I know exactly what it feels like to lie awake at night and not be able to turn that sound off. I know what it’s like when a doctor tells you, “Learn to live with it.” And I know what it’s like to feel completely alone with a problem nobody can see.

When I developed tinnitus for the first time in 2011 after going to a club, it was the beginning of an odyssey through ENT practices, forums, and sleepless nights. Four different ENT doctors. Not one of them could help me. So I started doing my own research—thousands of hours, over the course of years. I dove deep into cell biology, cochlear physiology, neurology, and nutritional biochemistry.

In the end, I found my own way. My tinnitus disappeared—completely, as confirmed by audiometry. Years later, I deliberately brought on a second, separate case of tinnitus to test my model. The tinnitus disappeared that time too—noticeably faster.

I created this page because I can’t keep this knowledge to myself. Not every path works for every person. But if my experiences and research help even one person with tinnitus find hope again, then every hour of work on this page was worth it.

Understanding Tinnitus: The Three Main Causes

Not all tinnitus is the same. Based on my experience and research, there are three fundamentally different triggers—and each one requires a different approach.

Noise-Induced Tinnitus

Clubs, concerts, acoustic trauma, chronic noise exposure—when the ear has been mechanically overloaded, the hair cells are stuck running on emergency power. Here I explain the entire mechanism at the cellular level: what happens at the molecular level, why the sound remains, and why conventional masking therapy can only go so far.

Stress-Induced/Psychosomatic Tinnitus

Not all tinnitus comes from the ear. Chronically unresolved inner conflicts can create sustained electrical tension in the nervous system that irritates the auditory system from within. Here I describe how that works and which approach helped me personally with the stress-induced component of my own tinnitus.
