Important to know

The niacin flush—no need to panic

When you take the B-vitamin complex with niacin, the so-called niacin flush can occur after about 10–15 minutes: warmth, often a slight tingling or reddening of the skin. This is a known, well-documented reaction to niacin—it briefly widens the blood vessels. Don’t let it scare you—the effect is normal, harmless, and fades on its own after a short while.

Easing in

If you’d like to ease into the concept cautiously at first, you can start with the B-complex alone. B vitamins support normal energy metabolism.
